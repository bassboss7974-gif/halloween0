01 — INTRODUCTION

What is Halloween?

Halloween is an annual celebration observed on October 31. It is known for costumes, carved pumpkins, haunted houses, and traditions that embrace the mysterious side of the night.

However, Halloween is more than just a night of costumes and candy. Its roots reach back thousands of years to ancient Celtic traditions and the festival known as Samhain.

02 — THE ORIGINS

A History Through Time

From an ancient Celtic festival to a worldwide cultural celebration, Halloween has evolved through centuries of history and tradition.

2000+ Years Ago

The Festival of Samhain

Ancient Celtic communities celebrated Samhain at the end of the harvest season. They believed this was a time when the boundary between the living and the dead became thin.

8th Century

Christian Traditions

November 1 became All Saints' Day in Western Christianity. The evening before became known as All Hallows' Eve, which eventually developed into the word Halloween.

19th Century

Halloween in America

Irish and Scottish immigrants brought Halloween customs to North America. The celebration gradually became more community-focused, with parties and seasonal activities.

20th Century

The Modern Celebration

Trick-or-treating, costumes, pumpkin carving, and neighborhood celebrations became increasingly popular.

Today

A Worldwide Celebration

Halloween is celebrated in many countries through costumes, decorations, entertainment, themed events, and creative traditions.

03 — TRADITIONS & CULTURE

The Halloween Traditions

Explore the symbols and traditions that make Halloween unique.

Jack-o'-Lantern

Carved pumpkins are one of Halloween's most recognizable symbols. The tradition developed from older practices of carving turnips in Ireland and Scotland.

Costumes

Dressing up has historical connections to customs associated with disguising oneself during seasonal festivals and later became a popular form of celebration.

Trick-or-Treating

Children visit houses wearing costumes and ask for treats. This tradition evolved from older European customs of going door to door for food and offerings.
